Lateral habenula

One-line definition: The lateral part of the habenula in the epithalamus, which receives limbic and basal ganglia inputs and inhibits dopamine neurons and sends negative-value signals to dopaminergic and serotonergic systems, signaling negative motivation and the absence of reward.
