Transient Receptor Potential Ankyrin 1 (TRPA1)

One-line definition: TRPA1 is a non-selective cation-permeable TRP ion channel expressed mainly in sensory neurons that responds to cold, irritant chemicals (such as mustard oil and acrolein), and tissue-damage signals to mediate pain, itch, and inflammatory signaling.
