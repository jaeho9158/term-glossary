Term list (EN samples) > 40 Hz sensory stimulation

40 Hz sensory stimulation

One-line definition: A noninvasive approach that uses 40 Hz flickering light or sound to induce gamma oscillations in the brain, mainly investigated in Alzheimer's disease research.
