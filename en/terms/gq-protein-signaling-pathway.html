Gq Protein Signaling Pathway (Gαq/PLC-β Pathway)

One-line definition: A signaling cascade in which activation of a Gq-coupled receptor releases the Gαq subunit to activate phospholipase C-beta (PLC-β), generating IP3 and DAG that trigger intracellular calcium release and protein kinase C activation.
