integrated Alzheimer's Disease Rating Scale (iADRS)

One-line definition: A combined rating scale for Alzheimer's disease trials that merges the Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale-Cognitive subscale (ADAS-Cog) and the Alzheimer's Disease Cooperative Study instrumental Activities of Daily Living scale (ADCS-iADL).
