DTI-ALPS (diffusion tensor image analysis along the perivascular space)

One-line definition: An index derived from diffusion tensor imaging that expresses water diffusion along the direction of perivascular spaces at the level of the lateral ventricle body, used as a proxy for glymphatic function but with debated interpretation.
