Glutamate Transporter-1 (GLT-1 / EAAT2)

One-line definition: GLT-1 (EAAT2) is a sodium-dependent membrane transporter, expressed mainly on astrocytes, that clears glutamate from the synaptic cleft to terminate excitatory neurotransmission and prevent excitotoxicity.
