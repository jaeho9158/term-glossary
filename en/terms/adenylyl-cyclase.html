Adenylyl Cyclase (Adenylate Cyclase, AC)

One-line definition: An enzyme that converts ATP into cyclic AMP (cAMP) and pyrophosphate, serving as the key second-messenger-generating enzyme in G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) signaling.
