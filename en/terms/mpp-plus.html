MPP+ (1-methyl-4-phenylpyridinium)

One-line definition: 1-Methyl-4-phenylpyridinium, the toxic bioactivation product of MPTP, which enters dopaminergic neurons and inhibits mitochondrial complex I, producing parkinsonian degeneration.
