Normal pressure hydrocephalus

One-line definition: A form of communicating hydrocephalus in which excess cerebrospinal fluid enlarges the ventricles while measured CSF pressure stays normal or slightly elevated, typically causing gait disturbance, cognitive decline and urinary symptoms.
