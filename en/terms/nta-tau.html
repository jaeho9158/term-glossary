N-terminal containing tau fragments (NTA-tau)

One-line definition: A biomarker measured with an ultrasensitive immunoassay (NTA) that targets N-terminal containing tau fragments in cerebrospinal fluid and plasma, studied as a marker closely linked to tau deposition in Alzheimer's disease.
