Preclinical Alzheimer Cognitive Composite 5 (PACC5)

One-line definition: A composite cognitive score that combines dementia screening, episodic memory, processing speed, and category fluency tests to capture subtle cognitive decline in the preclinical stage of Alzheimer's disease.
