Subharmonic signal (ultrasound contrast microbubbles)

One-line definition: A nonlinear scattered signal that ultrasound contrast microbubbles emit at half the transmit frequency (1/2 f0); it is hard to generate in tissue and is used for contrast imaging and pressure estimation.
