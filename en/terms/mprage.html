MPRAGE (magnetization-prepared rapid gradient echo)

One-line definition: An MRI sequence that applies a magnetization-preparation pulse followed by a rapid gradient-echo readout to produce 3D T1-weighted images with clear contrast among gray matter, white matter, and lesions.
