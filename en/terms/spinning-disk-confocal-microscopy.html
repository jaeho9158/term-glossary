Term list (EN samples) > spinning disk confocal microscopy

spinning disk confocal microscopy

One-line definition: A confocal microscope that uses a rotating disk with many pinholes to scan many spots of light in parallel and records in-focus fluorescence with a camera, enabling fast imaging with low phototoxicity.
