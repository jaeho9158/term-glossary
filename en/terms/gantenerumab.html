Term list (EN samples) > Gantenerumab

Gantenerumab

One-line definition: A fully human IgG1 anti-amyloid-beta monoclonal antibody with highest affinity for aggregated amyloid-beta, given subcutaneously; it lowered plaque burden in phase 3 trials without slowing clinical decline.
