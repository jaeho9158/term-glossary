POMC neurons (proopiomelanocortin neurons)

One-line definition: Neurons that express the proopiomelanocortin (Pomc) gene, located mainly in the hypothalamic arcuate nucleus, and that help reduce food intake and raise energy expenditure when energy is plentiful.
