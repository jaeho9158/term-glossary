Limbic-predominant age-related TDP-43 encephalopathy (LATE)

One-line definition: A neurodegenerative disease of older adults in which abnormal TDP-43 protein deposits accumulate predominantly in limbic structures such as the amygdala and hippocampus, causing memory impairment resembling Alzheimer's disease.
